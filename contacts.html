District 39

District 39 is reached through its trusted servants at the monthly meeting (third Sunday, 4:00 p.m. — time and place). In keeping with A.A.’s tradition of anonymity this site does not publish personal names or phone numbers; current officers are named by first name in the minutes.

DCM (District Committee Member)
Carries the district’s voice to Area 59; questions about GSO group registration and the Fellowship Connection listing.
Alternate DCM
Reports from NEPA Intergroup; requests for literature at a group event. Election in November 2026.
Treasurer
Group contributions and the monthly treasury report. Election in November 2026.
Secretary
Minutes and corrections to this site. Election in November 2026.
Workshop chair
District workshops, including the Traditions workshop being planned.
Grapevine, Visitation and Marathon chairs
Reports at the monthly meeting.
