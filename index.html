District 39 of Alcoholics Anonymous

District 39 is a general service district of Area 59 (Eastern Pennsylvania) in Northeastern Pennsylvania. General Service Representatives (GSRs) from local A.A. groups meet once a month to share news, carry their groups’ conscience to the Area, and bring information back home.
