Old Business

  • Emily's motion from last month came back for a vote. It was brought to the floor in June and groups were asked to discuss it.

    I motion that our district express concern regarding the process of the recent change to the upcoming Fifth Edition of the Big Book and request that the Area Committee and Delegate review the process by which the change was made, with particular attention to whether sufficient opportunity was provided for group conscience input.

    Further, I motion that we recommend that future changes to A.A. literature, service materials, or other matters affecting the Fellowship and the A.A. message must be communicated to and discussed by the groups through their GSRs prior to delegates taking positions or casting votes whenever practicable, so that the group conscience remains the foundation of our service structure.

  • The motion passed on the first vote with one opposed. The chair asked for the opposition's reasons before proceeding.
    • The motion would hold the district to the same standard it is asking of Area. Getting twenty groups to agree on a topic for a workshop, for example, would be difficult, and we should think about whether we will practice what we preach.
    • It runs against Concept Three, the Right of Decision. The spiritual principle of Concept Three is trust. A trusted servant who is in the room, who hears the arguments and the testimony, has the right to cast his vote accordingly even when the group has told him something else.
    • The Conference vote was overwhelming, well over two thirds — roughly 122 in favor
    • Other countries have already removed the word from the Big Book. This happens.
    • The procedure they followed was in keeping with the Traditions and the Concepts
  • Russell noted that four or five years ago the Conference voted that changes to the Big Book have to come back to the groups; that has already been set and agreed to, and the delegates changed it without coming back to the groups.
  • It was asked by a member whether a copy of A.A.'s bylaws is available. Trusted servants are trusted until they aren't, and going against the group conscience of the group you agreed to represent is what undermines that trust.
  • The motion was voted on again after the opposition was heard. It passed well over two thirds, with two abstentions.
  • The district then took up whether to send the motion to Area or directly to GSO. Area can decline to move it up or send it to a subcommittee, where it can be buried.
    • A motion was made to send it directly to GSO: "I hear what you're saying, but it's like saying my vote doesn't count. How we vote matters. It's important that we make our voice heard, whether they pay attention or not."
    • The motion to send it directly to GSO was seconded and passed.
  • It will play out the way it is going to play out, but at least Area and GSO will know where District 39 stands.
