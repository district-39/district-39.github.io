DCM (Russell)

A motion from nine districts. Russell read a motion that nine districts have put forward. We have talked about this here.

Motion to have all current DCMs and area officers submit a written service resume prior to EPGSA for voters to reference during Area elections.

The background information submitted with the motion, in full:

I am making this motion after speaking with several people about the voting process at EPGSA and discussing ways to make it more informative for GSRs during elections. The motion was brought up at our last district meeting. Only one GSR in attendance had previously attended EPGSA during a voting year. We did not take a formal vote, however, the discussion that ensued supported having something more in place to make informed decisions during our elections.

When I served as a GSR, I often had little to no knowledge of the individuals standing for Area Panel positions. In many cases, I either asked others who they were voting for or selected a name at random, especially if that person appeared to be leading. I know I am not the only person who has experienced this.

As a DCM, I encourage GSRs to attend Area events so they can become familiar with trusted servants and learn more about the people involved in service. However, the reality is that attendance at these events is often limited. During elections, the voting body is frequently provided with little more than the names of those standing for positions. This does not give GSRs enough information to make fully informed decisions and is not the most effective way to elect trusted servants to the Area Panel.

I have reached out to several Areas regarding their election procedures, and this is what I have found:

The following Areas require candidates to give a verbal service resume when standing for a position: Areas 11, 12, 15, 30, 44, 45, 49, 60

There is currently a motion pending in Area 15 to post service resumes on their website during elections. They are voting on the motion at their next area meeting. It is the only Area I have reached out to that may potentially present a written service resume for the body to reference prior to or during elections.

I have not yet reached out to, or heard back from, additional Areas, but I am continuing to gather information on how other Areas facilitate their elections.

Due to the length of our election process, I believe that pre-submitted service resumes may be more beneficial and time-efficient than having individuals give verbal resumes during elections.

Russell will reach out to Area 15 to find out what they are doing and what the intent is, and whether an amendment could allow the body to talk with candidates.
