DCM Report (Russell)

Russell reviewed the General Service Conference report. The confidential full report is not attached to these public minutes.

  • The Conference approved publication of the Fifth Edition of the Big Book, with 26 new personal stories and updated appendices.
  • Seventh Tradition contributions in 2025 were $10.579 million, 6% below 2024. The report covered operating revenue and expenses and efforts to reduce costs.
  • Nearly 85,000 literature items were accessed through tablets in correctional facilities during 2025, a 300% increase over the previous year.
  • The literature price increase discussed in the report took effect February 1; it was not announced as a new increase at this meeting.
  • Grapevine reported reduced losses and a projected financial break-even in 2028.
  • Alex, Area 59 alternate delegate, reported that A.A. World Services had acquired the Everything A.A. app. Licensing and content were being reviewed; further discussion was expected at the General Service Board's October meeting.
  • The suggested Area contribution toward a delegate’s expense to attend the Conference increased from $2,200.00 to $3,000.00.
  • The Conference voted to discontinue Equitable Distribution of Workload, which redistributed agenda items among Conference committees.
  • Area Day is Saturday, October 24, in Tannersville; please see the flyer for details.
