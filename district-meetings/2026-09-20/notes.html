Guest — EPGSA Convention Committee (Tate)

Tate, treasurer of the EPGSA convention committee, is visiting districts to encourage attendance.
  • This is a voting year. Concept Nine: if you don't vote, we don't have a chance
  • Registration is $25.00, online or by mailing the form. You get a confirming email. The hotel is booked separately
  • Ask whether your group can afford to send you. Stay at the hotel if you can; on a voting year things run late. Room nights also pay for the meeting space, and the committee owes a fee if the block is not filled
  • Same hotel next year; other venues are being looked at for the year after
  • Coming only Saturday to vote? Be there by 8:00 a.m. Attendance is counted at 8:45 and again thirty minutes later; you must be present for both. After the second count the district's votes are fixed for the day
  • Groups and districts can contribute to the hospitality suite by check or through the Area website
  • Volunteers are needed; pick your time slots on the sign-up. Not just for GSRs. EPGSA 2026 Volunteers Needed Flyer
