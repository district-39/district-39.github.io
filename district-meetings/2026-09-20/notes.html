Guest — EPGSA Convention Committee (Tate)

Tate is treasurer of the EPGSA convention committee and a past DCM. The committee is visiting districts to stress the importance of attendance and raise awareness of EPGSA.
  • This is a voting year. Concept Nine: if you don't vote, we don't have a chance. Participation is what makes representation accurate, and it is how the right people end up in the chairs to carry the message to the alcoholic who still suffers
  • Registration is $25.00, online or by mailing the form to the address printed on it. You will get an email confirming your registration. Registration and the hotel are separate; the hotel handles its own bookings.
  • Ask whether your group can afford to send you. If you can stay at the hotel, stay at the hotel: on a voting year things run late and you are right there. The room rate also pays for the meeting space the committee contracts for, and the committee owes a fee if the room block is not filled.
  • The convention will be at the Wyndham again next year. The committee is looking at other venues for the year after.
  • If you are only coming Saturday to vote, be there by 8:00 a.m. Attendance for voting is counted at 8:45. There are two counts about thirty minutes apart and you must be present for both; after the second count the district's number of votes is fixed for the rest of the day, no matter who arrives later.
  • Groups and districts can contribute to the hospitality suite by check or online through the Area website.
  • Volunteers are needed for the weekend. The sign-up lets you pick the time slots you are free. The conference is not just for GSRs. EPGSA 2026 Volunteers Needed Flyer
  • If you are going, three months out is when it should be on your radar
