District 39 monthly service meeting

Third Sunday of each month, 4:00 p.m.

Check the location before you go. Two public listings disagree:

  • Area 59 district list: Lackawanna County Building, 205 W. Grant Street, Olyphant, PA 18447
  • NEPA Intergroup “Districts We Serve”: 1000 Taylor Avenue, Scranton, PA

Ask a District 39 GSR or call the Intergroup office (570-654-1226) to confirm.

Upcoming dates

  • Sunday, October 18, 2026 — Area panel members invited to take questions.
  • November 2026 (third Sunday is November 15) — district elections for secretary, treasurer and alternate DCM.

Dates follow the third-Sunday pattern; when a meeting moves, the change is announced in the minutes.

Who comes

Each group’s GSR (or alternate) attends to report for the group and to take district, Area and Intergroup news back to it. The meeting covers the treasury report, committee reports (workshop, Grapevine, visitation, marathon), the DCM’s Area report, an Intergroup report, group concerns, and old and new business.
